# Decline — this is an archive, not a system

I was pointed at a folder and asked for a map. I will not draw one.

## What is here
A pile of unrelated files: a resume, a tax PDF, a few photos, a folder of receipts. Real things,
but each one stands alone.

## Why there is no map to draw
The nouns **do not move each other.** Changing the resume does not touch the tax PDF; deleting a
photo moves nothing else. With no movements, every card's "what else moves" line would be empty.
