## What it is
One-click checkout that charges the shopper and clears their bag. The map claims this is wired
and in force (roadmap.md:4).

## Why it is shaped this way
Payment lives in one place so no route re-implements charging.

## Hits — change this, these move
- the bag — checkout clears it after a successful charge.

## Does not hit — the wrong neighbour
- the price table — checkout reads the computed total; it does not set prices.
